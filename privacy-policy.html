True North: Data Stewardship

Last Reviewed: February 2026

1. Our Commitment

At True North, we believe your spiritual journey is sacred. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your reflections remain your own.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Email and name when you sign up.
  • Reflections: Your journal entries are encrypted and used only to provide Spiritual Intelligence guidance at your request.
  • Preferences: Your selected belief system and themes to personalize your experience.
  • Camera & Photos: We access your camera and photo library only when you choose to take or upload a profile picture. Images are stored securely and not used for any other purpose.

3. Spiritual Intelligence & Analysis

When you use the Spiritual Intelligence feature, your journal entry is processed by our secure spiritual intelligence partner (Google Gemini) to generate guidance. This data is not used for training models or shared for advertising.

4. Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time through the Privacy & Security settings in your profile.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for secure data storage and RevenueCat for managing premium subscriptions. These partners adhere to strict privacy standards.

Your trust is our foundation.
