# Google Play Console Setup for Internal Testing

This guide explains how to set up your app on the Google Play Console and distribute it to testers using the **Internal Testing** track.

## Prerequisites
- **Google Play Developer Account**: You must have a developer account ($25 one-time fee). [Sign up here](https://play.google.com/console/signup).
- **App Bundle (.aab)**: The file generated by running `eas build --platform android --profile production`.

## Step 1: Create the App
1.  Log in to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2.  Click **Create app** (top right).
3.  **App Details**:
    - **App Name**: `True North` (or your chosen name).
    - **Default Language**: English (US).
    - **App or Game**: App.
    - **Free or Paid**: Free.
4.  **Declarations**: Accept the Developer Program Policies and US Export Laws.
5.  Click **Create app**.

## Step 2: Set up Internal Testing
1.  In the left menu, go to **Testing > Internal testing**.
2.  Click **Create new release**.
3.  **App Integrity**: If asked about Play App Signing, click **Choose signing key** and select **Use Google-generated key** (recommended for new apps).

## Step 3: Upload the App Bundle
1.  Under **App bundles**, click **Upload**.
2.  Select the `.aab` file you downloaded from the Expo build dashboard.
3.  **Release Name**: Give it a version name (e.g., `1.0.0 (Internal Release)`).
4.  **Release Notes**: Add a short description for your testers.
5.  Click **Next**.

## Step 4: Review and Rollout
1.  You might see warnings (yellow) or errors (red).
    - **Errors** must be fixed before you can proceed.
    - **Warnings** can often be ignored for internal testing.
2.  In the **Preview** screen, review the details.
3.  Click **Start rollout to Internal testing**.

## Step 5: Add Testers
1.  Go back to the **Internal testing** tab (Testing > Internal testing).
2.  Click the **Testers** tab (next to Releases).
3.  **Create email list**:
    - Click **Create email list**.
    - Name the list (e.g., "Internal Team").
    - Add the email addresses of your testers (they must use these emails on their Android devices).
4.  **Select the list**: Check the box next to your new list to activate it.
5.  **Copy Link**: Under "How testers join your test", verify that specific instructions are set.
    - Click **Copy link** (Join on the web).
    - Send this link to your testers. They **must** accept the invitation via this link before they can download the app from the Play Store.

## Troubleshooting
- **Package Name Error**: Ensure the package name in `app.json` (`com.thashiznitt.truenorth`) matches what you might have drafted if you created the app manually before without uploading.
- **Version Code Error**: If you upload a new build, ensure the `buildNumber` (Android `versionCode`) is higher than the previous one. EAS handles this automatically with `"autoIncrement": true`.
