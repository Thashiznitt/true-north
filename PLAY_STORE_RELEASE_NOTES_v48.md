# True North Release Notes
**Version Name:** 1.0.0
**Version Code:** 48
**Release Date:** April 2026

## What's New & Improved

### Secure Freemium Architecture 
- **Fair Access Guarantee:** We’ve officially rolled out an inclusive freemium limit guaranteeing all seekers can utilize our core features natively. Users on the free tier can securely create up to 5 brand new Sanctuary Reflections or interactively join up to 5 public circles without ever hitting a hard lock.
- **Data Loss Prevention:** We've totally rewritten the interceptor walls. Instead of randomly losing data mid-save because you reached your limit, the app now perfectly anticipates if you are out of free entries and cleanly brings up the RevenueCat paywall right as you hit the "+ New Journal" button, mathematically ensuring you never waste time typing something you can't securely save. 
- **Universal Reading Access:** We have dramatically un-gated the Sanctuary view. Non-subscribers can now comfortably and peacefully read their entire past journal history and freely look through the global Active Circles feed indefinitely.
