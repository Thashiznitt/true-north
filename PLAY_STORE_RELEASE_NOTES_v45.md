# True North Release Notes
**Version Name:** 1.0.0
**Version Code:** 45
**Release Date:** April 2026

## What's New & Improved

### Seamless Spiritual Intelligence
- **Humanized Reflections:** The Spiritual Intelligence generating perspectives within your assigned circles now communicates entirely in profound, flowing, human-shaped prose. Robotic hyphenated lists and disruptive bullet points have been completely removed for a deeply natural experience.

### Fortified Security & Privacy
- **Universal Biometrics Lock:** Your circles are now automatically safeguarded! If you’ve enabled Biometrics (FaceID/TouchID) in settings, the app lock will seamlessly trigger your specific native biometrics instead of defaulting to the manual 4-digit PIN fallback.
- **Robust Identifiers:** During registration and onboarding, spaces are now systematically prevented during handle generation to ensure all user references and @tags remain perfectly intact.

### Interface & Quality of Life
- **Ascending Global Transient Notifications (Toasts):** Removed disruptive 'Alert' popups from throughout the application. When sharing blessings to a circle, syncing your belief alignments, or updating your cosmic themes, True North will now display an elegant, smooth 'rising' Toast notification from the bottom edge of the screen—confirming your action organically without halting the flow of your journey.
- Addressed multiple underlying state concurrency optimizations surrounding the PIN gate clearing logic and session locking integrity.
